Brass components, made to your drawing

AirGrip makes brass parts to customer drawings in Jamnagar, India: turned components, forgings, fittings, moulding inserts, electrical parts, special fasteners and stampings, plus a standard range of brass cable glands and accessories. Founded in 1988 by Ketan Shah.

Turned parts
CNC and cam-automat turned, Ø5–100 mm, ±0.05 mm; ISO 2768-m unless the drawing states otherwise
Forgings
Hot-forged, then machined, 25 g – 1.0 kg
To drawing
Fittings, moulding inserts, terminal blocks and neutral links, special fasteners, brass, copper and steel stampings
Cable glands
Clamping-jaw and strain-relief glands, pressure screws, lock nuts, adapters, stop plugs, earth tags; PG 7–48 and M12–M80; EPDM seal, IP54
Volumes
From a few thousand pieces to millions a year

How we make it

Material
Rod bought from approved mills; CW614N and CW617N as standard; low-lead, lead-free and DZR grades on request
Machine park
12 CNC lathes, 20 cam automats, 21 special-purpose machines built in-house, 10 drilling machines, 3 presses
Processes
Hot forging, turning, drilling, threading, milling, stamping, plating, sub-assembly
Threads
Metric, PG, BSP and BSPT, NPT, UNC/UNF, BA, BSW; special threads to drawing
Plating
