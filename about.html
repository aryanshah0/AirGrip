How we work

AirGrip started in 1988 making custom brass forged and turned parts in Jamnagar, the centre of India's brass-parts industry. It is led by its founder, Ketan Shah.

Over that time we've built our own fixtures and mechanisms to take manual handling, and with it errors, out of repeat jobs.

We buy rod from approved mills and do the forging, machining, plating and inspection in our plant; some material tests are done at outside labs. How each batch is checked and traced: Quality.
