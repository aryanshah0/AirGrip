# AirGrip — Photo & video shot list (for the professional shoot)

Goal: replace every "Current photo · reshoot planned" image on the new site with consistent, honest, high-quality photography
of AirGrip's real parts, plant and people. **No stock photos.** Buyers check whether photos are real.

## Brief for the photographer
- **Style:** clean, technical, natural. Think precision-catalogue (Swiss/German supplier), not glossy advertising.
- **Parts:** shot on **one** neutral background, either seamless light-grey paper (#E8E8E4-ish) or matte white acrylic. Never thermocol, cloth or wood.
  Soft, even light with one gentle highlight to show the brass surface and machining marks. Show threads sharply.
- **Format:** RAW plus high-res JPEG, at least 4000 px on the long side. **Landscape 4:3** for products (plus a 1:1 crop safe zone in the middle).
  Leave space around parts so the site can crop.
- **Colour:** neutral white balance. Brass must look like brass (warm gold), not orange or yellow. Nickel-plated parts in cool silver.
- **Plant:** natural daylight plus work lights, wide and medium shots, real work in progress, safety gear on. Tidy the area first.
- **People:** real employees only, with consent. Leadership portraits against a neutral wall plus one "at the machine" shot each.
