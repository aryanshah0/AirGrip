# Page guide: what each page is for and what it shows

Plain-language guide for reviewing the design. For each page: who it's for, the job it does, what's on it (top to bottom),
and what is still waiting on AirGrip (question numbers refer to `QUESTIONS-FOR-KETAN.md`).
Benchmarks used in the content review: Paloma and Jakap (Jamnagar competitors), Geissel, Rajhans, Vaishnavi, VAP, and
JACOB, Lapp and Hummel (cable-gland catalogues).

## Content review: what changed (no owner input needed)
- Home: capabilities now come before traceability. The repeated export lines in the plant block are removed.
- Exports page: renamed "Ordering & shipping", given a "Buying within India" block (India is the first market), and added to the main menu.
- Capabilities: commercial terms moved out of the packing section.
- Industries: standards worded as "your drawing may call these up" (they are not AirGrip approvals).
- Quote form: no invented tolerance; plating thickness field; documents-required ticks (PPAP, 3.1, RoHS/REACH, lead-free); how to send a physical sample.
- PG chart: PG → metric replacement table added.
- Contact: LinkedIn tile removed until the URL exists; plant-visit row added. 404: phone and contact link.
- Gland pages: prominent review note about the JACOB product names (owner decision, Q1–8).

- Coverage review (old site vs new): 6 group pages, 2 gland pages, 8 accessory pages and 4 technical pages built from the old site's content, so nothing the old site offered is lost; links rewired everywhere (menu, footer, hubs, home, industries).
- Wording: "family-run" removed; cluster line now "brass rod extrusion plants within about 3 km" (old site); forging finish "shot-blasted, natural or bright"; Favorit "bending protection"; quality adds near-zero-defect target and training; capabilities adds design-for-cost; About names every product group.
- Kompakt: old "A/C" values were bar-stock (rod) sizes, not an error; held back until confirmed (B4). BSP/NPT removed from Kompakt options (glands are PG/metric; inch threads are for accessories).
- Ordering & shipping: Incoterms table collapsed behind a "Show" toggle; nav label now "Ordering & shipping" everywhere.
