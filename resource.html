PG thread chart

The PG thread (Panzergewinde) is a German conduit thread with an 80° flank angle and a shallow profile. It's still found on many cable glands, enclosures and replacement parts, although new designs mostly use metric threads to EN 60423.

Standard
DIN 40430
Flank angle
80°
