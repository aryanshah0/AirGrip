How we work

AirGrip was founded in 1988 in Jamnagar, the centre of India's brass-parts industry, and is led by its founder, Ketan Shah.

We buy rod from approved mills and do the forging, machining, plating and inspection in our plant; material tests are done at an outside lab. How each batch is checked and traced: Quality.
