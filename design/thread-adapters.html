Three ways to change an entry thread

Each part joins two threads: the enclosure entry and the gland. Pick both; the part code records the pair.

Reducers

Step a larger enclosure entry down to a smaller gland thread, without re-machining the enclosure.

Code: AG-RD-[entry][gland]-[finish], one quote line per pair. Finish is chosen in the quote form. Dimensions with your quote.

Enlargers

Take a smaller enclosure entry up to a larger gland thread.

Code: AG-EN-[entry][gland]-[finish], one quote line per pair. Finish is chosen in the quote form. Dimensions with your quote.

Adapters

Convert an entry from one thread system to another, e.g. PG to metric, or NPT to metric.

Code: AG-AD-[entry][gland]-[finish], one quote line per pair. Finish is chosen in the quote form. Dimensions with your quote.
