How to review

  1. Open each page on a laptop and on your phone.
  2. Try the flows: Products menu → Pressure screw → + Add two sizes → Lock nuts → + Add → Review & send → quote form → Send.
  3. TBC = data AirGrip must supply. Dotted gold underline = wording to confirm or correct.
  4. Line drawings are interim imagery until the photo shoot. Hatched "shot A1/B6" boxes are photos from the shot list.
  5. Send changes as: page → section → what to change.
