§ 02 · From melt to measured part

Our own rod, under one roof.

Most turned-parts suppliers buy their rod. We cast and extrude ours, so alloy, cost and lead time stay in our control, not a mill's. Eight stages, one plant, one batch record.

01Foundry

Cast

Brass melted and cast to billet in-house.

CuZn39Pb3 · CW614N
02Lab

Spectro check

Every melt's chemistry verified before it becomes rod.

Cu 56–59 % · Pb 1.5–3.5 %
03Press

Extrude

Billet extruded to round, hex or profile rod.

Round · hex · profile
04Draw bench

Draw & straighten

Rod drawn to size and straightened for the bar feeder.

Size & straightness checked

Machine
