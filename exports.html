Ordering questions

For every product group and the gland range.

What do you need for a quote?
A drawing (PDF is fine; STEP helps), the material, finish, quantity per order and expected annual volume, and for exports the destination port or city. If you only have a sample, send it and we'll work from it.
Is there a minimum order quantity?
It depends on the part and process. CNC runs can be small; cam-automat parts are economical from larger batches. The quote states the MOQ.
How long do samples and production take?
Samples typically take 2–3 weeks, with an inspection report. Production is made to order and follows in 4–6 weeks after you approve the samples. Every quote confirms the lead time.
Can you sign an NDA before we send drawings?
Yes. Tick "Sign my NDA before I share drawings" on the quote form and send us your NDA; we sign it before you share drawings.
Which quality documents come with a batch?
See Quality: what you receive. Shipping documents are listed above.

Sourcing brass parts from India?

Send a drawing with quantities, your Incoterm and the destination port.
